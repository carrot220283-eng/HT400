# 停住的怀表（THE STOP WATCH）

原书：p.248 ｜ 章：WATCH TRICKS（手表魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：WT-001

道具：怀表、磁铁
难度：入门
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

怀表在手中滴答走着，放在桌上却立刻停住。

## 方法

1. 在桌布下面藏一块磁铁。
2. 展示怀表，让观众听见滴答声。
3. 把怀表放到桌上、正对磁铁的位置，机芯会被磁力吸住而停走。
4. 不要用贵重的表做这个戏法，磁铁可能损坏它。
5. 最好用一只便宜的旧表，尤其是一只走时本来就不准的表。

## 提示

磁铁位置要对准表，千万不要用贵重怀表冒险。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

A watch is shown, with the second hand running. Anyone can hear the watch tick. But when the watch is laid on the table, it stops immediately. Method Have a magnet concealed under the table-cloth. When the watch is laid above the magnet, the works will be stopped. Never perform this trick with an expensive watch, as the magnet may affect it. Use an old, cheap watch; especially one that is an erratic time keeper.

# 数字六（NUMBER SIX）

原书：p.248-249 ｜ 章：WATCH TRICKS（手表魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：WT-002

道具：一块表
难度：入门
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师问过对方手表上的六是罗马数字还是阿拉伯数字，对方一看表，却发现表面上根本没有六。

## 方法

1. 先开口问对方现在几点。
2. 对方把手表放回口袋时，说他每天都看表，却说不清表面上的六是 VI 还是 6。
3. 对方说自己当然知道，并说出答案。
4. 让他当场看表，表面上根本没有数字六。
5. 原因是秒针的位置占据了原本该写六的地方。
6. 问他时间，就是为了先确认这只表有没有秒针。

## 提示

必须先确认表上有秒针再开玩笑，语气不要显得在考问。

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/wt-002-1.jpg)
![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/wt-002-2.jpg)

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

This is a catch, rather than a trick. Ask a person the time, and when he replaces his watch in his pocket, mention that although he has been looking at his watch every day, he cannot tell you whether the number six on the face is a Roman numeral (VI) or an Arabic numeral (6). Of course he will say that he does know; and when he has stated which it is, tell him to look at his watch. To his surprise he will find that there is no number six at all! The reason is because the second hand of the watch occupies the space provided for number 6. When you ask him for the time your purpose is to learn whether or not his watch has a second hand. If it has one, you may go ahead safely.

# 读出时间（TELLING THE TIME）

原书：p.249 ｜ 章：WATCH TRICKS（手表魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：WT-003

道具：一块表、桌子
难度：进阶
时长：3 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：需要

## 效果

观众把表拨到任意带五点的时间并扣在桌上，魔术师只看表背就说出时间。

## 方法

1. 借一块表，请观众拨到任意时间，如某点后五、十、十五分钟。
2. 魔术师离开房间，表被扣在桌上。
3. 与同伙事先约定：把桌面划成十二个假想的方格，每格代表一个钟点。
4. 同伙趁乱把表移到代表该小时的那一格。
5. 同时转动表，让表冠指向分钟数，仿佛表外有一圈表盘。
6. 魔术师回来看一眼表的背面，读出表冠的位置便知道时间。

## 提示

十二格的位置与表冠指向的约定要事先练熟，同伙移动时要装得随意。

原页截图：
![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/wt-003-1.jpg)

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Borrow a watch and tell the company to set it at any time they choose, as five, ten, fifteen (or some other unit of five) minutes after an hour. You leave the room while this is being done, and the watch is laid face down on the table. When you return, you merely look at the back of the watch and immediately tell the time at which it is set! Method: You must have a confederate for this trick. You and he divide the table into twelve imaginary squares, each square representing an hour. After the watch has been set and placed on the table, the confederate carelessly moves it into the proper square to designate the hour. At the same time he turns the watch so that the stem points to the minute, imagining that there is a dial around the watch. One glance at the watch will tell you the time at which it is set.

# 神秘的闹钟（THE MYSTIC ALARM CLOCK）

原书：p.249-250 ｜ 章：WATCH TRICKS（手表魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：WT-004

道具：闹钟
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

观众把闹钟拨到任意分钟、面朝下扣在桌上，魔术师只看钟背就说出了分针指的数字。

## 方法

1. 请观众转动分针，调到任意数字，再把闹钟面朝下放好。
2. 翻开钟背，观察上发条的旋钮。
3. 旋钮上常有一个小记号，当分针指十二时它就朝钟的上方。
4. 若没有记号，可先把钟拨到十二点，在旋钮上划一道刻痕。
5. 从旋钮刻痕的位置就能读出分针所指，相当于一个反向的小表盘：12、11、10、9……

## 提示

刻痕要事先做在对着十二点的位置，观察时别让观众看到你的视线。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

This a similar trick with an alarm clock; but no confederate needed. Some person is invited to set the minute hand of the clock at any number he chooses, and to lay the clock face down. Looking at the back of the clock, you name the number to which the large hand points. Method: The winding knob of an alarm clock often has a little mark which points to the top of the clock when the minute hand points to twelve. If no such mark appears, set the clock at twelve and make a scratch on the knob. When you look at the knob it will tell you where the minute hand is pointing; for the scratch on the knob will act as an indicator on a tiny imaginary dial. The "dial" will be in reverse order, running 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, etc.

# 手帕里取出怀表（THE WATCH FROM THE HANDKERCHIEF）

原书：p.250 ｜ 章：WATCH TRICKS（手表魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：WT-005

道具：大手帕、金属环、怀表、餐巾
难度：进阶
时长：3 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：可选

## 效果

怀表包在大手帕中央，手帕四角穿过金属环由几位观众拉住；盖上餐巾再伸手进去，怀表竟被取了出来。

## 方法

1. 把怀表放在大手帕中央，四角穿进金属环。
2. 请几位观众各拉住手帕的一角。
3. 抛一块餐巾盖住手帕，手伸到布下。
4. 抓住手帕的一边把它从金属环里向下拉，四角之间便空出缝隙。
5. 从缝隙里把怀表取出来。
6. 若手上带一枚硬币，可以把它塞进手帕代替怀表，再把金属环拉紧贴住硬币。

## 提示

拉手帕时只拉一侧，缝隙要藏在餐巾底下，取表动作要干脆。

原页截图：
![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/wt-005-1.jpg)

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

A watch is placed in the center of a large handkerchief. The corners of the handkerchief are drawn through a metal ring, and the corners are held by different persons. Then you throw a napkin over the handkerchief, and reaching beneath, immediately draw out the watch! Method Although the corners of the handkerchief are held, and the watch cannot pass through the metal ring, the removal of the watch is an easy matter. Take hold of the side of the handkerchief and pull it down through the ring, thus making a space between the corners through which you may withdraw the watch. If you have a coin in your hand, you can drop it in the handkerchief instead of the watch. Then pull the ring down against the coin.

# 掉进袖子（DOWN THE SLEEVE）

原书：p.250 ｜ 章：WATCH TRICKS（手表魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：WT-006

道具：怀表、有胸袋的外套
难度：入门
时长：2 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

怀表被丢进左袖，袖子被抖动、摸索，里面却什么也没有。

## 方法

1. 把左手举到脸旁，让袖口正对左胸的口袋。
2. 右手拇指与食指捏住怀表。
3. 右手其余手指把袖口撑开，让怀表落进胸袋。
4. 放下左手，请大家在袖子里找表。
5. 抖动袖子让人摸，表早已在胸袋里。

## 提示

袖口要对准胸袋口，放表的动作要像真的往里丢一样自然。

原页截图：
![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/wt-006-1.jpg)

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Magicians are supposed to put objects "up their sleeves." In this trick you deliberately drop a watch in your sleeve, and then challenge anyone to find it. The sleeve is shaken, and felt, but no trace of the watch remains! Method: The watch is apparently put in the left sleeve. Hold your left hand against your face so that the opening of the sleeve comes directly in front of the left breast pocket. Hold the watch between your right thumb and forefinger, and as the other fingers spread the sleeve open, let the watch drop in the pocket. The illusion is perfect. Extend your hand, and shake the sleeve a bit, and everyone will look there for the watch.
