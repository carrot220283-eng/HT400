# 火柴盒里的硬币消失（MATCH BOX COIN VANISH）

原书：p.83 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-001

道具：一枚硬币、一只火柴盒
难度：进阶
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

硬币放进火柴盒、合上抽屉摇一摇还有响声，再打开时硬币却不见了。

## 方法

1. 事先在火柴盒内盒的一端剪一道小缝，大小刚好能让硬币通过。
2. 把硬币从缝隙塞进内盒，平时看不出来。
3. 把硬币放进盒里，合上抽屉，横向摇动，让观众听到硬币在里面响。
4. 把盒子朝自己这边倾斜、让剪缝那一头朝内，硬币就滑出来落进手里。
5. 另一只手接过盒子放到一边，稍后再打开给观众看。

## 提示

剪缝要开在盒角不显眼处；摇盒时只能横向摇，否则硬币会提前滑出。

When the box is shaken sideways, the coin will ratde; but as soon as you tilt the box towards yourself, with the trick end inwards, the coin will slide out into your hand. # 羊毛球里的硬币（COIN IN BALL OF WOOL）

原书：p.83-84 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-002

道具：一枚做过记号的硬币、一团羊毛球、一个能让硬币滑过的硬筒
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

刚才从火柴盒里消失的硬币，最后从一团羊毛球中间被拆出来，当啷一声落进玻璃杯。

## 方法

1. 用金属或硬纸做一个扁筒，让硬币能从里面滑过。
2. 把羊毛绕着这个筒卷成一个球。
3. 你已经把火柴盒里的硬币藏到手后，转身去拿羊毛球，把硬币从筒里放进去。
4. 抽出筒，把羊毛球拿到观众面前，放进一只玻璃杯。
5. 请人把羊毛球拆开，硬币就落进杯里。

## 提示

要用做过记号的硬币；若用松散的羊毛球，也可以直接把硬币塞进羊毛再揉紧，不必用筒。

After "vanishing" the coin in the manner explained, you can reproduce it from the center of a ball of wool. # 戒指盖走硬币（RING AND VANISHING COIN）

原书：p.84 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-003

道具：直径约一英寸半的戒指、一张纸、一块方形硬纸板
难度：入门
时长：1 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

戒指放在纸上，用纸板一盖；把戒指罩到硬币上再掀开纸板，硬币不见了；再盖一下，硬币又回来了。

## 方法

1. 在戒指底部粘一片与桌面垫纸同色、和戒指一样大的圆纸片。
2. 把戒指放在纸上，圆纸片看起来就是纸的一部分，没人会发现。
3. 用一小块方形硬纸板把戒指盖住。
4. 把戒指罩到硬币上，再掀起纸板，硬币被圆纸片挡住，看起来消失了。
5. 再把戒指盖住、拿开，硬币就又出现。

## 提示

圆纸片的颜色必须和垫纸完全一致；这招太有名，最好只当作下一招的引子。

# 懂行人也看不出（THE "WISENHEIMER" COIN TRICK）

原书：p.84-85 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-004

道具：戒指、一块方形硬纸板、一小块木头、一条与纸板同宽的硬纸板、蜡或铅膏
难度：进阶
时长：1 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

纸板一盖一掀，硬币又消失了；但自称懂行的人拿起戒指检查，却发现戒指底下根本没有粘纸片。

## 方法

1. 准备一条和方形纸板一样宽的硬纸板，在其末端粘一小块木头，做成能随纸板一起掀起的托盘。
2. 在木头底面抹一点蜡或铅膏。
3. 把纸板盖到戒指上，再连戒指一起罩到硬币上，让木头粘住硬币。
4. 掀起纸板，硬币被木头带走，顺手把它放进自己口袋。
5. 「懂行」的人拿起戒指检查，发现没有纸片，反而更加困惑。

## 提示

木头底面要够粘又不留痕；掀纸板时保持水平，别让硬币晃动出声。

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/cn-004-1.jpg)
![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/cn-004-2.jpg)

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

The effect of this trick is identical to that of the last. A cardboard is set over a ring; the ring is set over a coin; when the cardboard is lifted, the coin is gone. Some "wise" person picks up the ring but finds that there is no paper pasted to it, after all! Hidden underneath the cardboard you have a little piece of wood, pasted to a strip of cardboard that is as wide as the cardboard square. Thus the little wooden affair can be lifted up with the square of cardboard. The bottom of the wooden piece is dabbed with wax or lead plaster. When the cardboard square is set over the ring, and both are put over the coin, the wooden piece picks up the coin. When the cardboard square is lifted, the coin is taken away and is calmly dropped in the pocket. The person who "knows all about it" picks up the ring and is surprised.

# 三枚变六枚（THREE APPEARING COINS）

原书：p.85 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-005

道具：六枚硬币
难度：高手
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

一只手捏着三枚硬币，正反两面各看一遍都是三枚；把手一抖再摊开，三枚竟变成了六枚。

## 方法

1. 六枚硬币一直夹在手里，靠叠放方式让人看起来只有三枚。
2. 一面叠三个正面、向外错开；另一面再叠两枚反面、朝相反方向错开，中间刚好给第六枚留出空间。
3. 拇指和食指捏住硬币边缘，先展示一面，说「这边三枚正面」。
4. 把手转过来展示另一面，说「这边三枚反面」，观众会以为前后是同样的三枚。
5. 抖一抖手，再把硬币摊开，六枚全部出现。

## 提示

叠放角度要精确；正反两面各看一次就收，别让观众盯着找破绽。

原页截图：
![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/cn-005-1.jpg)

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Three coins are held edgewise between the thumb and forefinger of one hand. The coins are overlapping and they are shown on one side— three heads— and on the other side— three The tails. hand shakes the coins, and when it is opened, the three have multiplied to six. The six coins are there all the time, but the method of holding makes them look like three. On one side are three heads, overlapping outward; on the other two more coins— tails overlap outward in the opposite direction. This allows space in between for the sixth coin. When you show one side, and remark, "Three coins heads on this side, and" (turning the hand around) "three tails on this side," every person will believe that he saw the same three coins each time.

# 弹回袖里的硬币（THE FADE-AWAY COIN）

原书：p.85-86 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-006

道具：半美元硬币、带孔金属圆片、蜡或铅膏、松紧绳、安全别针
难度：高手
时长：2 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

硬币按在膝盖上方的裤腿上，用布一盖，再把布拉直，硬币竟然凭空消失了。

## 方法

1. 准备一枚小金属圆片，边缘打一个孔，抹上蜡或铅膏，把松紧绳系在孔上。
2. 松紧绳沿右袖往上，末端用安全别针固定；金属片平时挂在袖口上方、外套袖子里。
3. 表演时把金属片从袖中拉出，粘在半美元硬币上；硬币夹在指尖，手背朝观众。
4. 右手把硬币按在膝盖上方的裤腿上，左手把一些裤布折起来盖住硬币。
5. 保持站姿；硬币一被盖住，右手就松压，硬币被松紧绳弹回袖子。
6. 双手抓住翻起的裤布往下折，再把布拉直，硬币已经不见了。

## 提示

松紧绳的弹力要够；盖布与松手必须在同一瞬间完成。

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/cn-006-1.jpg)

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

This is a very clever vanishing coin trick. A half dollar is used; and beside the coin you require a small disc of metal, with a hole punched near one edge. The disc is smeared with wax or lead plaster and a piece of cord elastic is hitched to the hole in the disc. The elastic runs up the right sleeve and terminates in a safety-pin. The metal disc should hang just above the cuff, inside the coat sleeve. To perform the trick, draw the disc from the sleeve and affix it to the half dollar, which is held at the tips of the fingers. The back of the hand is turned to the audience. The right hand places the coin on the trouser leg, just above the knee, and holds it there, while the left hand folds some of the cloth up over the coin. A standing position must be assumed. As soon as the coin is covered, the right hand releases pressure and the coin flies up the sleeve. Then both hands take hold of the
upturned cloth and bend it downwards. Then the cloth is pulled out straight, and the coin is gone.

# 旋空消失的硬币（THE WHIRL-AWAY COIN）

原书：p.86 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-007

道具：一枚硬币
难度：进阶
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

硬币在空中一旋被右手接住，拍在左手手背上；等观众猜完正反，抬手一看，硬币根本不在。

## 方法

1. 把硬币在空中旋转抛起，右手去接。
2. 接的时候手指伸直、手背朝观众，其实并不接，让硬币落进外套袖子里。
3. 立刻握拳，装作已经接住，并把「硬币」拍在左手手背上。
4. 问观众：是正面还是反面。
5. 等对方猜完，抬起右手，展示硬币根本不在手里。

## 提示

袖子要宽一点；拍手背的力度要和真的拍着硬币一样。

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/carrot220283-eng/HT400@main/img/cn-007-1.jpg)

This is a quick little trick that requires some practice. coin is whirled in the air and is caught in the right hand, which slaps it on the back of the left. "Which is it," you ask, "heads or tails?" As soon as the guess has been made you lift your hand and show that the coin is not there at all. In "catching" the coin in the right hand, you must hold your fingers straight up, with the back of the hand towards the audience. As the coin apparently falls into your hand, you do not catch it, but let it drop into your coat sleeve. You close your fist, however, and pretend to slap the imaginary coin on the back of your left hand. This is a very effective illusion that is not difficult to perform after a time.

# 掌心换硬币（THE CHANGING COIN）

原书：p.86-87 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-008

道具：一枚二角五分硬币、一枚五分镍币
难度：高手
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：可选

## 效果

观众把二角五分硬币摊在掌心，魔术师用指尖一击，他立刻握拳，摊开时掌心的硬币却变成了五分镍币。

## 方法

1. 事先把五分镍币夹在右手小指弯曲处。
2. 请观众摊开手，把二角五分硬币放在他掌心。
3. 你用指尖用力击打他的掌心，同时伸直小指，让五分镍币落下。
4. 这一击会把二角五分震得弹起，你顺手把它带走并收回手。
5. 观众以为自己握住了硬币，摊开却发现是五分镍币。

## 提示

击掌、松币、收手要在瞬间完成；只求快和准，不必用力过猛。

This is a feat of dexterity that will require careful practice, until the knack is acquired. A person is invited to hold out his hand, flat, with a quarter on the palm. 道具：若干硬币

1. 请观众抓几枚硬币握在拳里。

4. 让观众数清自己的硬币，报出奇偶。
5. 把魔术师的硬币加进去，总数果然按预言互换了奇偶。

## 提示

自己必须始终拿奇数枚：奇数加奇数得偶数，偶数加奇数得奇数。

# 吸在门上的硬币（THE COIN ON THE DOOR）

原书：p.87-88 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-011

道具：一枚二角五分或半美元硬币、一枚普通硬币
难度：进阶
时长：1 分钟
场合：任意
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师把硬币往门上一按，松手后硬币竟贴在门上不掉，好像木头有磁性。

## 方法

1. 取一枚二角五分或半美元硬币，在边缘并排锉出两个小缺口，中间留下一个微小的凸点。
2. 把凸点按在门上，硬币就平贴在木面上。
3. 缺口几乎看不出来；把硬币碰落到地上，捡起时换成另一枚普通硬币交给观众检查。
4. 也可以一开始就借观众的硬币，转身走向门口时把做好缺口的硬币换上去。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Four coins all alike are laid on the table, forming a square. Two playing cards are used, and a coin is covered with each card. Taking an uncovered coin, the magician puts it under the table, or the table-cloth, and when a card is lifted, two coins are there. The two coins are covered, and the other uncovered coin is passed under the card in the same mysterious manner. Then the three coins are covered. The other card is lifted, and the coin has passed from beneath it, all four coins being found beneath one card. This trick must be carefully rehearsed; but it is not difficult to perform. At the outset, the magician is undecided which coins to cover with the cards. He tries the cards over different coins, and this gives him the opportunity to take hold of a coin beneath the card in his right hand. He lifts the left hand card from a coin, and just as he draws the right hand card away, he drops the left hand card where the right
one was, so that nobody notices the absence of the coin. Then he lays the right hand card over another coin, taking care that the coins do not clink. He now has two coins under one card and no coin under the other. He picks up an uncovered coin, and in putting it under the table or the cloth transfers it to his left hand. He brings out his right hand, empty, and with it lifts the card, disclosing two coins. The card is transferred to the left hand which is below the level of the table, the coin resting on the left fingers. Then the left hand lays the card on the two coins, adding the third coin to the group. In the same way the other uncovered coin is "passed" beneath the card, and is added to the group when the card is replaced. That brings four coins beneath one card, while the other card covers none. So the magician makes a mysterious pass, lifts both cards, and shows all the coins together.
