# 简单的四 A 魔术（AN EASY FOUR ACE TRICK）

原书：p.55-57 ｜ 章：CARD TRICKS（纸牌魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CT-034

道具：四张 A、纸牌一副
难度：进阶
时长：4 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

四张 A 被发成四堆、每堆压上三张牌，观众选走一堆后翻开，四张 A 竟然全都聚在剩下那一堆里。

## 方法

1. 把四张 A 与另外三张杂牌排成扇形展示：前三张 A 后面藏三张杂牌，最前面再放最后一张 A。
2. 把后面那三张杂牌整理齐，使它和前面那张 A 看起来像一张牌。
3. 把“四张 A”放到牌堆上，整理齐。
4. 把顶上的“四张 A”一张张发成一行，其实只有第一张是真 A。
5. 发牌时让一张真 A“不小心”从牌堆里掉出来，马上捡起，让观众瞥见它的牌面。
6. 在每张“假 A”上各发三张牌，最先发出的三张正是那三张 A，四张 A 便都聚到同一堆。
7. 请观众说一个 1 到 4 之间的数字：说二就从左数、说三就从右数，都会点中四 A 那堆；说一或四就请他另说一个数字。
8. 让观众把手按在选中的那堆上，逐一打响其余几堆，最后翻开那堆，四张 A 都在里面。

## 提示

全程用夸张的表演制造神秘感，观众的注意力就不会落在牌上。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Everyone has heard of the "Four Ace Trick", and here is an easy way to present this mystery. The four aces are shown in a fan and are replaced on the pack. They are then dealt in a row, and three cards are dealt on each ace. One ace and its three odd cards are chosen. The magician snaps the other piles and throws them on the table. The aces are
gone, and they are found in the odd pile all the aces together. The first important part of this trick is the method of exhibiting the aces. They are held in a fan faces toward the audience; but behind the third card from the front are situated three indifferent cards. That is, the fan might be: ace of spades, ace of hearts, ace of diamonds, three odd cards, and ace of clubs. The three extra cards are squared up, however, so that they and the ace in front of them appear to be one card. The four aces are exhibited in this fashion, and are placed on the pack and squared up. Then the top four cards are dealt in a row. They are supposed to be the aces, as each card is dealt face down; but only the first card is an ace. In moving the cards around a bit, the real ace should be allowed to fall from the pack, accidentally, being immediately picked up, the audience, however, catching a momentary glimpse of its face. Three cards are dealt on each supposed ace, the first three cards from the top of the pack going on the real ace. Those three cards are the three aces; thus the four aces come together, while each of the other heaps holds four indifferent cards. The four heaps are laid in a row, thus: Four Four Four Four Ordinary Ace Ordinary Ordinary Cards Heap Cards Cards. A spectator is asked to choose a number between one and four. This apparently gives him any choice, but it implies that he should say two, or three. If he says "two", the magician counts from the left of the line; if he says "three", the magician counts from the right, ending his count in either case upon the four ace heap. Should the spectator say "four", the magician replies: "We will use all jour heaps. Give me another number".
Should the spectator say "one", the magician replies: "We will choose one heap from the four. Let me have another number". But the original question invariably eliminates one and four. All that remains is to command the aces to pass, all four being found in the chosen heap. The magician should do this with much pretence, having a spectator place his hand upon the chosen heap, and snapping each of the odd heaps. He should create the impression that he is doing something really marvelous.
