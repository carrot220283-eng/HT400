# 线轴换位（CHANGING SPOOLS）

原书：p.159-160 ｜ 章：MISCELLANEOUS TRICKS（杂项魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：MS-002

道具：两个线轴（一黑一红）、细绳、与线同色的纸条、手帕
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

一黑一红两个线轴穿在同一根绳上，手帕一盖再揭开，原来在右边的红线轴跑到了左边。

## 方法

1. 准备两个线轴：一个绕黑线，一个绕红线；再准备与线色相同的纸条。
2. 用红纸把黑线包成紧筒，用黑纸包住红线；这样两个线轴看上去普普通通。
3. 把两个线轴穿在细绳上，两端请人拉住，再盖上手帕。
4. 在手帕下面把纸筒撕掉，把纸片随手帕一起拿走。
5. 揭开手帕，两个线轴的位置就「换」了；线轴和细绳都可以仔细检查。

## 提示

纸筒要贴得平顺，颜色必须和线完全一致，才看不出破绽。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Two spools, one containing black silk thread, the other red, are threaded on a string, and the ends are held. A handkerchief is thrown over the spools and the magician reaches beneath it. When he removes the handkerchief, the spools have changed their positions. The red, which was originally on the right, is now on the left. To perform this trick, obtain two strips of paper which match the thread on the spools. Cover the black thread with red paper, gluing it to form a tight cylinder; and cover the red thread with black paper. The spools will appear quite ordinary. When they are under the cloth, tear the paper coverings off the spools, and remove the papers with the handkerchief. Thus the spools will
apparently be transposed. Both the spools and the string may be thoroughly examined at the conclusion of the trick.
