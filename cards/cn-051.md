# 飞钉上天花板（TACK IN THE CEILING）

原书：p.106-107 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-051

道具：图钉、半美元硬币、薄棉纸
难度：进阶
时长：1 分钟
场合：任意
需要助手：否

## 效果

图钉被随手往天花板上一扔，竟稳稳钉进了天花板。

## 方法

1. 取一张很薄的棉纸。
2. 把图钉尖朝上放在半美元硬币上。
3. 用棉纸包住硬币，并把纸裹紧。
4. 把纸包用力朝天花板抛上去，保持钉尖朝上。
5. 图钉钉进天花板，硬币的重量随后坠破纸，连纸一起落下。

## 提示

抛掷要直上直下，钉尖必须朝上，棉纸要薄到能被硬币坠破。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

This does not start out as a coin trick but a coin plays the most important part in it. A thumb-tack is to be driven in the ceiling, simply by tossing it in the air. Everyone wants to see that done, so the magician obliges.
He takes a piece of very thin tissue paper. He lays the tack point up upon a half dollar; then he places the paper over the point of the tack and wraps the paper around the coin. The package is tossed forcibly against the ceiling, holding the point of the tack upward. The tack is thus driven into the ceiling; then the weight of the coin tears the paper loose and down drops the coin with the paper, leaving the tack up there.
