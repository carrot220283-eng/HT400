# 木桨魔术（THE PADDLE TRICK）

原书：p.57-58 ｜ 章：CARD TRICKS（纸牌魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CT-035

道具：芥末木桨、纸牌一副、微型牌
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

木桨被夹在牌堆中间，再掀开上半叠时，木桨上竟出现一张小牌，正是本来压在木桨上方的那张牌。

## 方法

1. 先做一张微型牌，用红黑墨水画即可，把它粘在木桨的一面；与它对应的真牌放在牌堆底。
2. 把牌堆切一下，展示木桨“两面”：翻面时用拇指和手指把木桨转一圈，实际同一面展示了两次。
3. 拿起下半叠牌，把木桨插在中间，再把上半叠放上去。
4. 插入时顺手把木桨转半圈，让粘着小牌的一面朝上，正好对着与它对应的那张牌。
5. 提走上半叠，木桨上就出现那张小牌，和掀开前看到的那张牌一致。

## 提示

翻桨时要在指间旋转，别让人看出只是同一面。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

The magician shows an ordinary mustard paddle which he lays on the table. A pack of cards is cut, and the paddle is laid between the portions of the pack, after both sides of the paddle have been shown. When the top part of the pack is lifted, a tiny card is seen on the surface of the paddle; and that card corresponds to the bottom card of the upper heap that is, the card directly above the paddle! The tiny card, which can easily be made with red or black ink, is affixed to the paddle in the first place. The card that corresponds to it is on the bottom of the pack. The pack is cut; then the paddle is shown apparently on both sides; but in turning the paddle over, it is revolved between the fingers and
thumb. (See page 37.) Thus one side of the paddle is shown twice. The magician picks up the lower half of the pac\, and places it on the upper, inserting the paddle between. As he does so, he gives the paddle a half turn, bringing the tiny card upward, directly below the card that corresponds to it. This trick has a very surprising conclusion; — it is not difficult to operate, as the paddle turning is very easy simply an adaptation on an old trick known as the "Jumping Peg Paddle". It is possible to obtain paddles with rounded handles for this trick, and they revolve very easily.
