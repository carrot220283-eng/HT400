# 穿透的纸（PENETRATING PAPER）

原书：p.189-190 ｜ 章：PAPER TRICKS（纸类魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：PR-024

道具：端部开口的信封、硬质彩纸、铅笔、丝带、小刀
难度：入门
时长：2 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师把彩纸装进信封，当着观众的面用铅笔和丝带穿透信封与纸，取出后纸却完好无损。

## 方法

1. 事先用锋利的刀在信封中央附近划一道缝。
2. 把彩纸从信封口推入，让它穿过那道缝，滑到信封另一面的内侧。
3. 对着光展示信封里确实有纸；左手指捏住信封，把纸轻轻弯离被刺的那一面。
4. 右手拿铅笔从信封正面刺入，看上去穿过了信封和纸。
5. 再拿丝带顺着同一个刺孔穿过去。
6. 抽出彩纸展开，纸完好无损；随后把信封撕开丢掉，撕的位置正好在缝上。

## 提示

刺入时左手必须把纸弯开，否则纸真的会被刺穿；信封用完即毁，不可重复使用。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

An envelope is used in this trick one of the type that opens at the end. Also a piece of stout, colored paper that is nearly as large as the envelope. The magician pushes the paper into the envelope, and holds the envelope in front of the light to show the paper within. Then, in plain view, he pushes a pencil through the envelope and the paper, and after it a piece of ribbon. But when he pulls out the sheet of paper, it is uninjured! Method: The envelope has a slit (cut with a sharp knife or safety-razor blade), near the center. When the paper is pushed in it goes through the slit. The other side of the envelope is held in view and objects are
pushed through by the right hand. The left fingers hold the envelope and they bend back the piece of paper so that it is not injured. Then the paper can be removed from the envelope and shown, while the envelope is torn and tossed away, because it is of no further use. The tear is made at the slit.
