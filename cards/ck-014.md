# 两叠棋子换位（CHANGING CHECKERS）

原书：p.76-78 ｜ 章：CHECKER TRICKS（跳棋魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CK-014

道具：一叠红棋、一叠黑棋、两张纸卷成的圆筒、两枚一面异色的特制棋子
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

红、黑两叠棋子各用纸筒罩住，相隔数尺放好；魔术师一声令下，掀起纸筒，两叠棋子的位置竟然互换了。

## 方法

1. 在两叠棋子的最底下各放一枚特制棋子：一枚上面是红色、底面涂黑；另一枚上面是黑色、底面涂红。
2. 把两叠棋子分别用纸卷成的圆筒罩住，筒口上端拧紧，从上方看不见棋子。
3. 把红叠和黑叠相隔数尺放在桌上。
4. 把两个纸筒混着转一圈，再借掀筒的动作歪起筒口，让人瞥见最底下那枚棋子的底面。
5. 这样黑叠看上去是红的、红叠看上去是黑的。
6. 假装下令让两叠互换，再掀起纸筒，观众看到的就是两叠棋子换了位置。

## 提示

特制棋子的底面颜色要涂得像真棋子；歪筒亮底的那一下要快而自然。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Two stacks of checkers are used in this trick one stack red, the other black. Each stack is wrapped up in a piece of paper, the paper being made into a cylinder which surrounds the stack, and the top being twisted over to hide the checkers from above. The red stack is placed several feet away from the black, and the magician commands them to change places. When he lifts the paper cylinders, the checkers have obeyed the order, the black being where the red were supposed to be, and vice versa. Two special checkers are required for this trick. One is red,
but with black on the bottom; the other is black, with the bottom colored red. It is best to have the bottoms painted the opposite color, but a circular piece of paper may be glued beneath each checker, instead. The prepared checkers are the bottom ones of the stacks. After each stack is surrounded with a cylinder, the magician closes the tops of the cylinders and mixes them around. Then, to learn which color is in a cylinder, he tilts up the cylinder and lets people glimpse the bottom of the lowermost checker. In this manner, the black stack is identified as red, and the red stack is supposed to be the black. The magician merely commands, lifts the paper cylinders, and shows the marvelous transposition.
