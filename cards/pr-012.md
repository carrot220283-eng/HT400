# 吹走纸片（BLOW THEM AWAY）

原书：p.182-183 ｜ 章：PAPER TRICKS（纸类魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：PR-012

道具：三张纸片
难度：入门
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

手背上放三张纸片，观众随便指定要吹走几张，魔术师一吹，飞走的正好是那几张。

## 方法

1. 把三张纸片平放在手背上。
2. 请观众指定吹走一张、两张或三张。
3. 用手指按住要留下的纸片。
4. 一吹气，没被按住的纸片飞走，被按住的纹丝不动。

## 提示

按住纸片的手指动作要小，吹气要突然。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Three pieces of paper are laid on the back of the hand. The magician states that he will blow away one, two, or three, as
required, the audience specifying the pieces to be blown away. But the others will not leave the hand. It seems impossible to blow away two pieces without disturbing the third or one without blowing the other two also. But the magician accomplishes the feat by placing the fingers of the other hand upon the papers designated to remain. Then when he blows, only the loose paper will fly away.
