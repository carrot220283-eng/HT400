# 卡片上的答案（THE MESSAGE ON THE CARD）

原书：p.187-188 ｜ 章：PAPER TRICKS（纸类魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：PR-021

道具：卡片、信封、宽橡皮筋、笔
难度：进阶
时长：3 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

卡片封进信封、套上橡皮筋，观众算出数字总和后打开信封，卡片上竟写着答案。

## 方法

1. 在信封正面中央开一道竖缝，宽度和橡皮筋差不多。
2. 把卡片从竖缝塞进信封，再用橡皮筋套住信封，把缝遮住。
3. 写一列各位数字，请观众把它们相加；同时你在信封上写某人的姓名缩写。
4. 趁观众计算，用左手拇指把橡皮筋拨开，在卡片上用很小的字写上总数。
5. 观众算完，揭下橡皮筋、撕开信封取出卡片，答案就在上面。
6. 你接过信封，随手从上到下撕开，把竖缝的痕迹毁掉。

## 提示

写总数要快、字要小，橡皮筋必须把竖缝遮严。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

A small card is placed in an envelope. The envelope is sealed and girded with a broad rubber band. Then some figures are written in a column. Someone is asked to add them up while the magician writes a person's initials on the envelope. He lets the person hold the envelope.
When the total of the figures is added, the person opens the envelope, and takes out the card. There is the answer on it! The envelope has a small vertical slit in the center of the face an opening that is almost as wide as the rubber band. The card is put in, and the band is put around the envelope, hiding the hole. The magician watches the column of figures which should be single figures, and mentally adds the total. While someone is adding up the total, he puts the initials on the envelope, and this gives him a chance to push the band aside with his left thumb and write the total in small figures on the card. The person who holds the envelope sees nothing wrong because of the rubber band. As soon as he has removed the card, by tearing the end of the envelope, the magician takes the envelope and carelessly tears it down the center, destroying traces of the opening.
