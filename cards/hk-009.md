# 眼前消失的手帕（THE DISAPPEARING HANDKERCHIEF）

原书：p.131-132 ｜ 章：HANDKERCHIEF TRICKS（手帕魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：HK-009

道具：小手帕
难度：入门
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师在观众鼻子底下把一小块手帕揉成团，双手一拍再摊开，手帕已消失得无影无踪。

## 方法

1. 用一块很小的手帕，把它紧紧揉成一个小球。
2. 双手合握，把手帕球举到观众鼻尖正前方。
3. 右手抓住球，双手略微抬高到观众眼睛上方，同时把右手腕拍在左手上。
4. 右手掌心正对观众，利用这一瞬间把手帕从观众头顶上方抛出去。
5. 立刻双手一拍，再摊开，两手空空。
6. 尽量把手帕抛到椅子或桌子后面，免得落在地上被看见。

## 提示

必须一次只给一个观众表演，距离越近越容易成功。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

This is a most mystifying trick which can, however, be shown to only one person at a time. Use a very small handkerchief. This you roll into a tight ball, and hold between your hands right in front of your spectator's nose. Suddenly you clap your hands,
and show them absolutely empty. The handkerchief has completely disappeared, right beneath his very eyes! The closeness of the trick is what makes it possible. The advantage is not with the spectator, but with you. When you are holding the handkerchief right in front of his face, take hold of it with the right hand, and raising your hands slighdy, just above his eyes, slap your right wrist against your left hand. The palm of the right hand is directly towards the spectator, and thus you project the handkerchief over his head so suddenly and so rapidly that he does not catch sight of it. Then you clap your hands and show them empty. If possible, throw the handkerchief so that it falls behind a chair or a table.
