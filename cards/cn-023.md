# 硬币瞬间消失（A QUICK COIN VANISH）

原书：p.95 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-023

道具：借来的硬币、带钩金属夹（文具店可购）
难度：进阶
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

借来一枚硬币拿在右手中，右手做向上抛掷的动作，硬币随即消失，摊开手空空如也。

## 方法

1. 借一枚硬币，把带钩金属夹夹在硬币上。
2. 侧身站立，让右侧避开观众视线。
3. 右手做出向上抛掷的动作。
4. 抛掷的瞬间右手垂到身侧，把连夹的硬币挂在外套上。
5. 再抬手作抛掷状，摊开右手：空的。

## 提示

挂钩要在垂手的一瞬间完成，动作必须像自然的抛接，不可停顿或张望。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

A coin is borrowed and taken in the right hand, which makes a throwing motion. The coin immediately disappears. For this trick, you require a hooked metal clip; several types of these clips are made, and they are sold in stationery stores for hanging up cards etc. The clip has a clamp which will hold the coin, and the hook is on the side. Borrow a coin, clamp the clip on it, and stand with your right side away from the audience. When you make the tossing motion, drop your hand to your side for an instant, and you can hook the coin on your coat without the least bit of hesitation. Then make the tossing motion and show the hand empty.
