# 海绵方块分体（THE RUBBER CUBE）

原书：p.163-164 ｜ 章：MISCELLANEOUS TRICKS（杂项魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：MS-011

道具：红色海绵橡胶方块、小剪刀
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师把一块红色海绵方块在两手间一搓，分开手时，每只手里各出现一块方块。

## 方法

1. 准备一块约两英寸见方的红色海绵橡胶方块。
2. 在侧面开一道缝，用剪刀把内部掏空，让它变成空壳；因为是海绵，开口看不出来。
3. 另取一块略小的方块塞进第一块内部；它能压得很小，两块可以当成一块展示。
4. 表演时把方块在两手之间搓动，里面的小块被挤出来。
5. 分开双手，两只手里各出现一块方块。

## 提示

内层方块要略小、能压缩，塞进去后从外面不能看出鼓包。

A cube of red sponge rubber is used in this clever mystery. It shown to the spectators; then the magician rubs it between his hands, and when he separates them, there is a rubber cube in each hand! The cube is nearly two inches square. It is made from a rubber sponge. Cut a slit in the side, and with a pair of small scissors, hollow out the interior of the cube. Thus the cube becomes a mere shell, but being made of sponge rubber, the opening will be unnoticed. Another cube, a trifle smaller than the first, is pushed into the first cube. It compresses to a very small size, and the cube within the cube can be safely shown as one. When the cube is rolled between the hands, the inner cube is forced out, and a cube appears in each hand.
