# 指间藏币（A QUICK COIN VANISH）

原书：p.103-104 ｜ 章：COIN TRICKS（硬币魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CN-045

道具：一角硬币（或便士）
难度：进阶
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

硬币夹在拇指与中指之间，手一抛硬币就消失了；空中一抓，它又回到手中。

## 方法

1. 把硬币放在中指上节的侧面，用拇指指尖按住。
2. 其余手指弯曲，只伸出食指，让观众看清硬币。
3. 消失时，把食指盖到拇指尖上向下压，再把拇指撤走。
4. 随即伸直手指、亮出手掌：硬币藏在指缝间，伸到手背后面。
5. 反向做同样的动作，硬币便重新出现。

## 提示

整套动作要在手快速抛挥的同时完成，结束时手指伸直、硬币已不见。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

This is an excellent method of vanishing a dime or a penny; it requires a slight bit of skill. The coin is held between the thumb and second finger. A toss
of the hand and the coin is gone! A clutch in the air and it is back again. Set the coin on the side of the second finger, on the upper knuckle, and hold it in position with the tip of the thumb. The fingers are bent in except the forefinger, which is extended so the coin may be seen. To vanish the coin, place the forefinger over the tip of the thumb; press down, and remove the thumb. Then straighten the fingers showing the palm of the hand. The coin is hidden between the fingers, and it extends in back of the hand. This must be done very quickly, while the hand makes a quick tossing sweep, ending with the fingers extended and the coin gone. A reversal of the movement brings back the coin.
