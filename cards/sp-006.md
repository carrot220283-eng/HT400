# 选中的答案（CHOOSING A QUESTION）

原书：p.207-208 ｜ 章：SPIRIT TRICKS（灵异魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：SP-006

道具：一只布袋（中间有隔层）、几张写着不同名字的卡片、一个信封
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

观众从布袋里随意抽出一张写着人名的卡片，上面的名字却正是魔术师事先封在信封里的那一个。

## 方法

1. 用布缝一只袋子，中间加一道隔层，把它分成两个格子。
2. 在一个格子里放几张写着同一个名字的卡片，另一个格子空着。
3. 当众把写着各种名字的卡片从明格放进去，撑开袋口时别露出暗格。
4. 把袋口转到暗格朝外，再请观众伸手进去抽一张。
5. 抽出来的必然是那张写着你想要名字的卡片；随后拆开信封展示预言。

## 提示

撑袋与转袋的动作要自然，不能让观众看到两个格子。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

In having messages appear upon slates, the magician must often decide upon something to write. For example, he can have
the audience choose the name of a great man, and then the name appears upon a slate. Or he can simply write something and seal it in an envelope; then he lets people select one of several cards with different names written thereon, and the chosen card will bear the name that the magician has written on the card in the envelope. But how is the proper card chosen? That is done by means of a small bag, in which the magician drops cards bearing different names. The bag is easily made at home. It is like two bags sewn together or better, an ordinary cloth bag with a cloth partition in the center. In one side are several cards all bearing the same name. When the magician holds the bag open, he spreads it so the secret side is not disclosed. The varying cards are dropped in, and the magician reopens the bag so that the secret pocket is now in view, and when anyone takes a card it must necessarily bear the desired name.
