# 自动上升的牌（THE AUTOMATIC RISING CARD）

原书：p.49-50 ｜ 章：CARD TRICKS（纸牌魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CT-025

道具：纸牌一张、锥形玻璃杯、干肥皂
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师把一张牌插进玻璃杯，呵令它升起来它却不动；他把牌取出在袖子上擦一擦再插回去，牌就自己从杯里升到他手中。

## 方法

1. 选一张表面光滑的亮面牌，杯子要用上宽下窄的锥形杯。
2. 用干肥皂在杯内壁擦出两条窄窄的通道，从杯口一直通到杯底，位置彼此相对。
3. 把牌插进杯里，让它的两个边缘正好落在两条光滑通道上，牌便会自动上升。
4. 若插的位置不对，牌就不会升。
5. 肥皂要擦得仔细均匀，让人看不出痕迹。

## 提示

插牌的位置是关键，多练几次记住角度，效果才稳定。

drinking-glass is employed in this trick also. Any card is taken from the pack and is pushed down into the glass. A spectator is told to ask the card to rise. The card, however, does not obey the command. The magician removes the card from the glass, strokes it on his sleeve, and inserts it in the glass, from which it immediately rises to his outstretched hand. Method: Use a smooth-finished glazed card, and a glass with tapering sides. Take a piece of dry soap and rub it on the inside of the glass, making two narrow channels at opposite points, running from the top of the glass to the bottom. When the card is pushed down into the glass so that its edges come in contact with the smooth paths, the card will rise; but if it is not pushed in at
the correct point, it will not rise. The soap should be applied carefully and evenly; then its presence will not be observable.
