# 牌盒里升起的牌（RISING CARD FROM CASE）

原书：p.62-63 ｜ 章：CARD TRICKS（纸牌魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CT-044

道具：一副扑克牌、牌盒
难度：入门
时长：1 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

牌装进牌盒后上下摇动，盒盖掀开着，一张指定的牌竟然从盒里慢慢升了起来。

## 方法

1. 先选定一张显眼的牌，比如 joker、黑桃 A，或观众刚才选的牌。
2. 把这牌放到牌堆里的某个特定位置，再把牌堆装进牌盒。
3. 翻下盒盖，握住牌盒上下摇动。
4. 停手后轻轻松开盒盖，那张牌就会从盒中升起来。
5. 事先用同一只牌盒试验，弄清它会升起哪张：底牌、倒数第二张，还是顶牌；以后把目标牌放在相应位置即可。

## 提示

每只牌盒会升起的位置不同，必须先试验再表演。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

By this method, the magician can make a playing card rise from the case. It can either be a joker, ace of spades, or some
outstanding card, or it can be a chosen card, which the magician has learned by the method just described. The pack is put in the case, and the case is shaken up and down, the cover being turned back. The desired card will rise. The trick is accomplished by simply shaking the pack up and down in the case. This action will cause a card to rise. The magician should experiment and discover which card rises in the particular case he uses. It may be the bottom card, the second from the bottom, or even the top card. He places the desired card at the proper position before he puts the pack in the case.
