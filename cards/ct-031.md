# 马耳他十字（MALTESE CROSSES）

原书：p.53-54 ｜ 章：CARD TRICKS（纸牌魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：CT-031

道具：纸牌八张（含小丑）
难度：进阶
时长：3 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师把八张牌摆成两个马耳他十字，请观众不断挑牌去牌，最后剩下的一张竟然是小丑。

## 方法

1. 把八张牌按两个马耳他十字摆好，牌面朝下，暗中记住小丑的位置。
2. 请观众从八张里挑出四张。
3. 若小丑在观众选的四张里，就拿走另外四张；若不在，就拿走观众选的四张，总之把小丑留在桌上。
4. 再从剩下的四张里请观众挑两张，用同样的办法把包含小丑的两张留下。
5. 最后从两张里请观众挑一张。
6. 若挑中的是小丑，就把另一张拿走，把小丑留在桌上；若不是，就拿走观众挑的那张。
7. 翻开最后一张，正是小丑。

## 提示

拿牌的动作要随意，像在配合观众的选择，而不是在做手脚。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

The magician states that he will play a joke on his audience. He arranges eight cards in two groups so that they form two Maltese crosses. Then he invites a person to choose any four of the cards one cross or the other; or four cards in a line or any combination that pleases him. This is done and the four cards are removed. From the four remaining cards, two are selected; and finally of the two that remain, one is chosen. The final card is turned up, and it proves to be the joker! This is artfully accomplished. The magician knows the position of the joker among the eight cards that are laid face down. If
the joker is in the four cards first chosen, the magician removes the other four cards. If it is not in the first four, the magician pic\s up the four that are chosen. He repeats this procedure with the four cards that remain, either leaving or picking up the two that are chosen. When the final choice is made, the magician picks up the card if it is not the joker; but if it is the joker, he removes the extra card, leaving the joker on the table. When this trick is performed in a nonchalant manner, its effect is very mystifying.
