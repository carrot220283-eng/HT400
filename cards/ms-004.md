# 三颗珠子（THE THREE BEADS）

原书：p.160-161 ｜ 章：MISCELLANEOUS TRICKS（杂项魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：MS-004

道具：红白蓝三颗珠子、铁丝环
难度：入门
时长：1 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

三颗珠子穿在一个拧死的铁丝环上，魔术师把环背到身后，再拿出来时，珠子已排成观众指定的顺序。

## 方法

1. 把红、白、蓝三颗珠子穿在一个铁丝环上，把环的两端拧死。
2. 请观众任意指定一种颜色顺序。
3. 把环放到背后，只需让一颗珠子沿着环滑动一圈，就能排成想要的顺序。
4. 把环从背后拿出来展示，顺序果然和观众指定的一样。

## 提示

红白蓝与白蓝红从两端数是一样的，记住这组等价关系。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Three beads are threaded on a wire ring, the ends of which are twisted so firmly that it would be impossible to remove the beads very quickly. In color, the beads are red, white and blue. The magician states that he will mysteriously arrange the colors in any order: red, blue, white; white, red, blue any order the audience may choose. An order is decided upon and the magician puts the ring behind his back. When he draws it forth, the beads are arranged in their chosen order! This trick is simple but effective. Most people will not realize it, but it is an easy matter to arrange the beads in any desired order by merely running one around the ring.
Red, blue and white is the same as white, blue, and red, according to which side the magician counts from. Red, white and blue is blue, white and red; and blue, red and white is white, red and blue.
