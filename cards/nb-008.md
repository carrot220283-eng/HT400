# 抢三十一（THE THIRTY-ONE TRICK）

原书：p.168-170 ｜ 章：NUMBER TRICKS（数字魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：NB-008

道具：纸条（36 张）、笔
难度：进阶
时长：5 分钟
场合：桌面
需要助手：可选

## 效果

两人轮流从纸堆抽条累加数字，谁先把总数凑到 31 谁赢；魔术师却总能抢先到 31。

## 方法

1. 把纸撕成 36 张小条，写上 1 到 6，每个数字各写六套，混在一起。
2. 和对方轮流从纸堆抽条报数，把抽到的数字不断累加。
3. 你先抽，第一张拿到 5。
4. 之后无论对方抽到几，你都抽一张与他相加等于 7 的纸条（他抽 1 你抽 6，他抽 2 你抽 5）。
5. 靠这个「凑 7」的办法控制总数，最后就能先到 31。
6. 熟练后也可以让对方先抽；他不知道窍门，早晚会把关键数让给你。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

Tear a sheet of paper into thirty-six slips and on each slip write the numbers from 1 to 6, making six sets in all. Now tell a person that you and he will take turns drawing slips from the pile, adding the numbers as you go along. The object of the game is to bring the total to exactly 31 and whichever person accomplishes that will be the winner. You will always be the winner because there is a trick to the game. This can best be understood by studying a sample game, the numbers — in italics representing the slips that you draw: 3 — 5 2 6 — / — 4 — j — 1 6 make the total (from your final slip) of 3/. All you have to do is bring the totals to the following keynumbers 5, 10, 1 j, 24 and finally 31. You start with a 5 and whatever the other player draws, you pick a slip which will make a total of 7 when added to his. Should he draw a 1, you draw a 6; when he takes 2, you pick 5 and so on.
As the first player, you are sure to win; but in introducing this trick, you can let the other player draw first. Not knowing the secret, he will generally give you a chance to strike one of the keynumbers prior to 31. After you have won several games, your opponent will want you to draw first, thinking it will be to your disadvantage, whereas actually it will be your clincher.
