# 拇指套穿手帕（THIMBLE THROUGH HANDKERCHIEF）

原书：p.236-237 ｜ 章：THIMBLE TRICKS（拇指套魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：TH-005

道具：手帕、两个拇指套（一大一小）
难度：进阶
时长：3 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

拇指套顶在手帕下面，用力一挤，拇指套竟穿透手帕露出半截；再一推，拇指套回到原处，手帕完好无损。

## 方法

1. 准备两个拇指套，一大一小，大拇指套能松松地套住小拇指套。
2. 把大拇指套的尖端剪掉，剪到粗糙部分的底部。
3. 大拇指套套在小拇指套上，短距离看仍像一只普通拇指套。
4. 展示拇指套，把它暂时放进左拳，其实把大拇指套的假尖端留在左手里。
5. 右手食指戴上真拇指套，伸到布下，把假尖端套上食指。
6. 此时展示手帕，看起来拇指套穿透了手帕。
7. 用左拳盖住右食指，取下假尖端，再抽走手帕亮出拇指套。
8. 把拇指套推进左拳并把假尖端留在上面，之后可双手空空地收尾。

## 提示

剪口要在粗糙底部、假尖端套上后看不出破绽，挤手帕的力度要稳。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

The magician borrows a handkerchief and places a thimble beneath it, holding the thimble on the tip of his forefinger. He squeezes the handkerchief, and the thimble goes half way through it, so that both sides of the cloth may be shown, with the thimble extending through. Then the thimble is pushed back, and the handkerchief is returned, uninjured. Two thimbles are required for the trick. One must fit loosely over the other. The tip of the larger thimble is cut off, just at the bottom of the rough part. When this thimble is placed on the smaller thimble, the thimble appears quite ordinary, at a short distance.
In working, show the thimble, and place it temporarily in the left Then take the thimble on the right forefinger, leaving fist. the false tip in the left hand. Place the right hand under the cloth, with the forefinger extended, and insert the forefinger into the left picking up the false tip. The handkerchief can then be shown fist, with the thimble apparently half way through it! Then place the left fist over the right forefinger and remove the false tip. Withdraw the handkerchief and show the thimble. Push the thimble into the left fist, and leave the false tip on it, so the hands may be shown empty, except for the thimble. It is an easy matter to dispose of the false tip after this.
