# 黑暗中的响声（STRANGE MANIFESTATIONS）

原书：p.210-211 ｜ 章：SPIRIT TRICKS（灵异魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：SP-012

道具：一只铃铛、一只号角、可以关灯的场地
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：舞台
需要助手：否

## 效果

魔术师坐在两人中间，两手分别被两人握住；灯一灭，号角、铃铛、敲击声接连响起，灯亮时一切如旧。

## 方法

1. 请两位观众分坐两侧：一人抓住魔术师的右腕，他用左手抓住另一人的手腕。
2. 关灯的一瞬间，他把两手迅速靠近，左手短暂松开左边那人的手腕，随即改用右手抓住它。
3. 这样他的左手就空出来了。
4. 左手在黑暗中吹号角、摇铃、敲出响声，声音全来自面前的桌上。
5. 亮灯前再把左手换回去抓腕、右手接回右腕，姿势与开始时一模一样。

## 提示

换手必须在黑暗最初几秒完成，抓腕的位置要事先摸准。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

The magician seats himself between two persons. He lets one person grip him by the right wrist, while with his left hand he
holds the wrist of the person on his left. Then he has the lights turned out. Immediately a horn is blown, a bell is rung, and raps are heard. The bell and horn are in front of the magician, but how can he operate them? Then the lights are turned on, and everything is seen as before. Method: Just as the lights are turned off, the magician draws his hands close together, and lets go of the person on the left for an instant, only to seize his wrist again. This time, however, he uses his right hand to take the person's wrist, leaving his left hand free to make the manifestations. Just before the lights are turned on he lets go with his right hand and grips the person's wrist with his left.
