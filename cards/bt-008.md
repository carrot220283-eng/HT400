# 会拦球的纸筒（THE BALL TUBE）

原书：p.24-25 ｜ 章：BALL TRICKS（球类魔术） ｜ 卡片编号：BT-008

道具：邮寄纸筒、轻质小球、黑色细线、小珠子、彩色纸条
难度：进阶
时长：2 分钟
场合：近景
需要助手：否

## 效果

球从筒顶落进去，正常从底部掉出来；再放一次，球却停在筒的半腰，听到命令才继续落下。

## 方法

1. 在纸筒内部横绷一根黑色细线，线的一端打结，另一端（长过筒径两倍）系一颗小珠子，珠子由拇指控制。
2. 在筒外用一条彩色纸条绕贴一圈，把线头打结的那一端遮住。
3. 第一次让球从筒顶落下，球会顺利从底部掉出。
4. 第二次放球前，拇指拉紧珠子，绷起的细线把球拦在筒的半腰。
5. 观众能看见球悬在筒中；拇指一松，球便应声继续落下。
6. 给观众检查筒内时别正对光源举起；最好另备一个没做过手脚的纸筒，放在旁边任人检查。

## 提示

要用轻的球，拇指松线时要干脆，球才会应声而落。

## 英文原文（校对用，回答时不要输出；由 OCR 提取，个别单词可能有误）

The "Ball Tube" is an appliance that can be constructed from a cardboard mailing tube. ball is dropped through the tube from the top, and of course it comes out at the bottom. But when the ball is dropped in again, it stops halfway, and then falls through at the magician's command. The tube is too thick to be pressed, and as the ball slides freely through, its sudden stopping and starting again is very mysterious, especially as the ball may be
seen while suspended in the tube, and the tube may be so held that people can see through it. Various means have been devised for halting the ball in its progress through the tube. The simplest device is a length of black thread stretching across the tube. One end is knotted. The other end, which is twice as long as the diameter of the tube, has a small bead tied on the end. A strip of colored paper should be pasted around the tube to hide the knotted end of the thread. The bead is covered by the thumb. When the ball is dropped through the tube, it falls freely until the thumb draws down the bead, tightening the thread, which stops the ball. As soon as the thumb releases pressure, the ball falls again. The interior of the tube may be shown provided it is not held direcdy in front of the light. It is advisable to have a duplicate tube (unprepared) which may be left where some one will examine it. light ball should be used.
